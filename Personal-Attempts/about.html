Who We Are

A Cave for the Curious

Cave Noire was born from a simple belief: that a truly great wine shop should feel like a discovery. Not intimidating. Not formulaic. Just honest, passionate, and alive with possibility.

We are the exclusive retail home of Dead Crow wines — one of Washington State's most exciting small-batch producers — and we've built our bottle shop around that same spirit of craft and intention. Every label on our shelves is here because we believe in it.

Located in Wenatchee, WA, we sit in the heart of one of the Pacific Northwest's most vibrant wine corridors. We're proud to serve our community and invite the world in, pour by pour.

Whether you're a seasoned collector or ordering your first glass, you belong here. Pull up a seat. We'll find you something perfect.

What We Stand For

Our Values

I

Intentionality

Every bottle we carry is deliberate. We taste, research, and curate with care — nothing ends up on our shelves by accident.

II

Community

We're rooted in Wenatchee. We champion local producers, host local gatherings, and believe wine is best when it brings people together.

III

Discovery

We love the unexpected pour — the grape you've never heard of, the region that surprises you. Adventure lives in the glass.

IV

Honesty

No pretension. No gatekeeping. We'll tell you what's great, what's interesting, and what might not be for you — because your trust matters more than a sale.
